With the truth exposed, Luca’s strategy shifted with terrifying precision. He didn't lash out blindly. Instead, he played the part of the helpless, wheelchair-bound patriarch while his trusted underground network hunted down the paper trail of the neurotoxin.

The trail led inexorably to one man: Victor Sterling, Luca’s former chief advisor and current head of operations, who had smoothly taken over the daily management of the empire when Luca lost the use of his legs. Sterling thought he had buried the evidence a decade ago. He thought Luca was a harmless ghost in a rolling chair.

While Luca coordinated his counter-strike behind closed doors, his physical therapy began in earnest. Under the guidance of a private, hand-picked medical team sworn to absolute secrecy, Luca started intensive neuromuscular rehabilitation. The nerves, dormant and starved of movement for twelve years, screamed in agony as they woke up. Every twitch, every microscopic flex of a muscle, was paid for in sweat and blinding pain.

Maya remained his most steadfast coach. She would sit on the edge of his therapy table, swinging her legs and chattering away, completely unaware of the deadly corporate war raging around them.

"See?" she told him one afternoon, watching his foot twitch in response to a nerve stimulator. "I told you they were just sleeping."

"You were right, little bird," Luca murmured, a rare, genuine smile touching his lips.

By early spring, Luca could stand for ten agonizing seconds with heavy braces and parallel bars. His muscles were weak, shaking with strain, but the connection was real. The paralysis was a lie.

The trap was set for the annual corporate board gala at the Reyes estate—a grand gathering where Sterling and his co-conspirators planned to officially vote Luca out of the remaining holding companies, declaring him medically incompetent.

The night of the gala arrived. The grand ballroom glittered with crystal chandeliers, filled with Boston and Chicago’s elite, unaware of the electric tension humming beneath the surface. Elena and Maya were safely tucked away in the private family quarters upstairs, watching the security feeds on a private monitor.

Victor Sterling stood at the podium, tapping his microphone to call the room to order. He wore a triumphant, predatory smile.

"Ladies and gentlemen," Sterling announced smoothly, addressing the crowd of hundreds. "We are here tonight to transition the full leadership of the Costello empire. For twelve years, our dear leader Luca has fought valiantly from his wheelchair, but time and biology have taken their toll. It is time for a new chapter under my guidance."

Murmurs rippled through the crowd. Sterling gestured toward the double doors at the back of the ballroom, where Luca’s motorized wheelchair typically rolled in.

Instead, the heavy oak doors swung open wide to the sound of absolute silence.
