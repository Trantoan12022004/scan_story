The boardroom at Vance Minerals headquarters in downtown Perth was designed to project raw, industrial dominance—paneled in dark timber and adorned with massive slabs of polished iron ore and raw lithium crystal mounted on display stands.

Marcus Vance sat at the head of the heavy oak table, crossing his thick arms over his chest as he studied us with unabashed scrutiny. "You want the Pilbara export corridors. You want control over the mineral shipments feeding half the manufacturing plants in Asia and Europe. And what makes you think I'd ever hand over my life's work to a pair of corporate raiders from New York?"

I didn't blink. I walked slowly to the edge of the table, placing a single, sleek electronic drive beside his coffee cup.

"We didn't come here to ask for your permission, Mr. Vance," I said quietly, the stillness in my voice carrying more weight than a shouting match. "We came to give you a choice. That drive contains the complete forensic audit of your underground tax evasion schemes, your unlicensed environmental dumping in the Kimberley region, and the bribed regulatory officials who have kept your mining operations afloat for the last decade."

Vance’s face hardened into a mask of stone. "You're bluffing. My security protocols are impenetrable."

"Your security protocols were written by software engineers who haven't updated their firewalls since 2024," Victor added coldly, leaning forward. "We downloaded your entire operational database three minutes before our jet touched down on your tarmac. By sunrise tomorrow, if you don't sign the integration agreement, those files go straight to the Australian Federal Police and the International Criminal Court."

Silence hung heavy in the room. Vance stared at us, the realization slowly dawning on him that he wasn't dealing with a standard corporate competitor. He was dealing with an apex predator.

Slowly, heavily, Vance reached out and picked up the gold pen resting beside the drive. Without another word, he pulled the papers toward him and signed his name across the dotted line, sealing the final corner of our global empire.

As we walked out of the building and stepped back into the brilliant, blinding sunlight of the Australian afternoon, the vast continent stretched out before us in all its endless, magnificent glory.

Victor reached out, taking my hand in his, our fingers locking together with absolute, unbreakable strength. The journey from the bleeding hands of a broken maid to the absolute summit of global power was complete. There were no more empires to conquer, no more enemies to defeat, and no more shadows to outrun.

"Well, my queen," Victor murmured, a deep, triumphant smile breaking across his face as we looked out over the world we had claimed with our own hands. "What shall we conquer next?"

I smiled back, my eyes shining with infinite possibility as I squeezed his hand tightly. "Nothing, Victor. We've won. The world is ours."
