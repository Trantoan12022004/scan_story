The conference parlor was a masterpiece of neoclassical design, featuring crystal chandeliers, hand-carved crown molding, and a long mahogany conference table surrounded by leather executive chairs. As Victor and I entered the room, Director Van Der Berg—a tall, silver-haired man with sharp, cautious eyes—instantly stood up from his seat, flanked by two junior advisors who looked visibly nervous.

"Mr. Costello. Mrs. Costello," Van Der Berg said, offering a stiff, respectful bow of his head as he extended a hand. "It is an honor to finally meet the minds behind the Castello global logistics network."

I stepped forward, my posture erect, my expression completely impassive yet unfailingly polite as I shook his hand with a firm, steady grip. "Welcome to New York, Director. I trust your flight across the Atlantic was comfortable?"

"Very comfortable, thank you," Van Der Berg replied, sitting back down only after Victor and I had taken our seats at the head of the table. He slid a thick, leather-bound contract folder across the polished wood toward us. "We reviewed your preliminary terms regarding the North Atlantic shipping corridors. While the rates are... aggressive, my board recognizes that stability in the current economic climate is paramount."

Victor leaned back in his chair, resting his arm casually along the armrest, his stormy gray eyes fixed on the foreign negotiator with a gaze that made the man swallow hard. "Stability isn't something we offer as a courtesy, Director Van Der Berg. It's a guarantee backed by absolute control. As long as our terms are met, your vessels will sail unhindered through every port from here to Rotterdam."

Van Der Berg nodded quickly, opening the folder and sliding a golden fountain pen across the table. "Then I believe there is nothing left to negotiate. Castello logistics dictates the terms, and we gladly accept."

I picked up the heavy gold-capped pen, signing my name with sharp, deliberate strokes at the bottom of the contract before sliding it across to Victor, who countersigned it with effortless authority. The deal was sealed—another cornerstone locked firmly into the foundation of our global empire.

As the meeting concluded and the delegates filed out of the room under the watchful escort of our security detail, Victor and I remained seated at the head of the long mahogany table. The morning sun had risen higher in the sky, flooding the magnificent study with brilliant, unblemished light.

I looked down at the signed contract, then raised my hand, watching the platinum signet ring gleam brightly beside the heavy gold band on Victor's finger. The journey from the cracked, bleeding hands of a desperate, forgotten maid to the absolute summit of power had been long, brutal, and unforgiving. But as Victor reached across the table to clasp my hand tightly in his, I knew there was nowhere else in the universe we were ever meant to be.

"Well, my queen," Victor murmured, a deep, triumphant smile breaking across his face. "What shall we conquer next?"

I smiled back, my eyes shining with infinite possibility as I stood up, ready to step into the brilliant, unshadowed future we had forged with our own hands. "The world, Victor. The world."
