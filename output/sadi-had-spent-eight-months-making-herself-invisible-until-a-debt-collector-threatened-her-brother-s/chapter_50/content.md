The morning sun rose over Lake Michigan with a brilliant, unblemished blaze of gold and amber, casting long, peaceful rays across the hardwood floors of the penthouse. There were no urgent comms chirping on the desk, no threat assessments waiting in the queue, and no shadow boards whispering in the dark.

I stood by the open balcony doors with a cup of black coffee in my hand, watching the gulls wheeling gracefully across the bright blue sky. Victor joined me a moment later, sliding his hand into mine, our fingers interlacing with the quiet, effortless familiarity of two souls who had crossed the fires of hell and emerged as sovereigns of the earth.

The journey from the bleeding hands of a forgotten maid to the absolute summit of global power was complete. Every battle had been fought, every victory claimed, every enemy silenced.

"What do you see out there, Elena?" Victor asked softly, nodding toward the endless horizon where the city met the sky.

I took a slow sip of my coffee, feeling the warm, steady rhythm of his hand against mine, and smiled with a peace that surpassed all understanding.

"Everything, Victor," I whispered. "Just everything."
