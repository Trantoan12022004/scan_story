The world below us hummed with the steady, unstoppable rhythm of an empire running at peak efficiency—a sprawling metropolis where every dock, street, and skyscraper bowed quietly to the authority we had carved out with blood, intellect, and unyielding will.

We stood together on the wide stone balcony of the master suite once more, watching the evening lights begin to flicker alive across the concrete and steel canyons of the city. The air was cool and crisp, carrying the faint, sweet scent of blooming roses from the gardens below.

There were no whispers of the past left to haunt us, no hidden threats lurking in the corners of our domain, and no shadows left to hide in. Every obstacle had been cleared, every rival subdued, and every dream we had forged in the darkest days of struggle had been realized in breathtaking clarity.

I rested my head gently against Victor’s broad shoulder, listening to the steady, reassuring beat of his heart as his arms wrapped securely around my waist, holding me against him like anchor and armor combined.

"What do we conquer next, my queen?" Victor whispered softly into my hair, his lips brushing against my temple.

I smiled, watching the first bright stars emerge in the deepening violet sky above the city we owned together.

"Nothing left to conquer, Victor," I murmured, turning slightly in his embrace to meet his gaze. "Now, we simply rule."
