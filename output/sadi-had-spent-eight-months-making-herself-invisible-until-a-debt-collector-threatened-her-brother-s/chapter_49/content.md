By evening, the rain had cleared, leaving behind a crisp, washed sky where the stars burned with diamond-sharp brilliance. We stood out on the high rooftop sanctuary once more, the city of Chicago humming its eternal, vibrant song far below us—a symphony of life, commerce, and human resilience.

Around us, the rooftop garden stood firm and unyielding against the night wind, protected by strength, loyalty, and truth. There were no more enemies to defeat, no more corporate conspiracies to unravel, and no more ghosts to outrun. There was only the present moment, stretching infinitely into a tomorrow we would build together.

I closed my eyes, letting out a long, relaxed sigh of absolute contentment.

“What are you thinking about now, CEO Vance?” Dorian—no, Victor—teased softly, his lips brushing against my temple.

I smiled, my eyes opening to gaze out across the endless horizon of the sleeping world. “I’m thinking that our executive decisions are finally complete.”

“Oh? And what decision is that?”

“Deciding that we never have to leave this rooftop again unless we genuinely feel like buying another continent.”

Victor burst out laughing, a rich, resonant sound that carried upward into the starlit sky, echoing across the peaceful rooftop garden.

“Deal,” he whispered, pulling me closer still as the city lights twinkled endlessly below. “The world can wait. Welcome home, my queen.”
