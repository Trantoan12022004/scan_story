By dawn, the underground vault was quiet once again, but the global machinery of the Castello syndicate was already operating at maximum velocity. High above the estate, automated satellite links transmitted encrypted instructions to our shipping hubs in Rotterdam, Singapore, and Los Angeles. We were no longer just a New York crime family; we were a transnational conglomerate hiding in plain sight, controlling billions of dollars in legitimate real estate, green energy initiatives, and international trade routes.

I stood on the wide stone balcony of the master suite, wrapped in a black silk robe while the morning mist rolled across the manicured lawns and private rose gardens. The pale sunlight caught the facets of my diamond ring and the platinum signet on my finger—twin symbols of absolute power.

Footsteps echoed softly behind me on the hardwood floor, followed by the familiar warmth of strong arms wrapping around my waist, pulling me back against a broad, solid chest.

"Penny for your thoughts, Mrs. Costello," Victor murmured into my hair, his lips brushing softly against the nape of my neck.

I leaned back into his embrace, smiling as I looked out over the sprawling empire we now ruled together. "I was just thinking," I said quietly, turning slightly in his arms to meet his stormy gray gaze, "that we need to audit the security protocols for the East River docks again."

Victor let out a rich, dark laugh, tightening his grip around me as he rested his chin on my shoulder. "Consider it done," he whispered. "The city is ours, Sadi. Let them try to break us."

Yet, even as we stood there in the quiet sanctuary of our estate, the digital terminal on the bedroom desk chimed with a high-priority encrypted alert. It wasn't a standard threat assessment or a routine shipping manifest. It was a ping from a dead-drop server in Zurich—a server that hadn't transmitted a signal in over a decade, tied directly to the vanished assets of my own family's past before the old Costello regime destroyed them.

My blood ran cold for a fraction of a second, but my expression remained an unreadable mask of iron.

"What is it?" Victor noticed the subtle shift in my posture immediately, his playful demeanor vanishing in an instant, replaced by the lethal focus of the syndicate boss.

I looked down at the flashing red light on the terminal across the room. "It seems, my love, that someone from my old life is finally ready to introduce themselves."
