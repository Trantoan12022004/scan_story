The annual Global Sovereign Gala was traditionally held in Geneva, but under the Vance administration, protocol was rewritten to suit their vision. This year, the gathering of the world's elite economic architects, technological innovators, and political figureheads took place in Chicago, transforming the Windy City into the undisputed capital of the new world order.

The grand ballroom of the Vance Global Tower was a masterclass in modern architectural grandeur—soaring ceilings of reinforced crystal, cascading water features, and panoramic views of a skyline illuminated by millions of LED nodes tied directly into the Vance AI network.

Elena descended the sweeping central staircase in a gown of midnight silk threaded with microscopic gold fiber that caught the ambient light like distant stars. Every eye in the cavernous room turned toward her. Hundreds of the most powerful individuals on earth—men and women who controlled trillions of dollars in sovereign debt and corporate equity—held their breath as she reached the floor.

Victor stood waiting at the base of the stairs, offering his arm with effortless grace. She placed her hand in his, and together they glided into the center of the room.

The applause began softly—a single, respectful ripple from the front row of European central bankers—before swelling into a deafening, thunderous ovation that shook the crystal chandeliers overhead. It was not the applause of sycophants; it was the sincere, reverent acknowledgment of subjects paying homage to their rightful sovereigns.

As the music resumed—a live string orchestra playing a complex neoclassical arrangement—Victor pulled her close for the opening dance.

"Look at them, Elena," Victor murmured near her ear as they moved effortlessly across the polished marble floor. "They smile, they toast to peace, they trade shares in our subsidiaries. Not a single one of them remembers what the world looked like five years ago, when it was tearing itself apart at the seams."

"They remember enough to be terrified of going back," Elena replied smoothly, her eyes scanning the crowd. She spotted representatives from rival factions who, years ago, had placed multi-million-dollar bounties on her head. Now, they fought for a five-minute slot on her calendar just to bow and offer subservience. "Fear is a powerful adhesive, Victor. But competence is what keeps it locked in place."

"And our competence is absolute."

"Precisely."

As the song ended, the crowd parted respectfully to allow the Prime Minister of the Unified European Federation and the Chief Executive of the Pan-Asian Tech Consortium to approach them. Both men bowed slightly—a gesture of profound respect that would have been unthinkable in the old political era.

"Madam Vance, Mr. Vance," the Prime Minister began, his voice tinged with nervous deference. "We have finalized the draft for the trans-continental trade harmonization treaty. All member states have ratified the framework without dissent."

Elena regarded him with a cool, measuring gaze. "Without dissent, Prime Minister? Or did your parliament simply realize that resistance is economically fatal?"

The Prime Minister swallowed hard, offering a strained, polite smile. "A combination of both, Madam Vance. The future is very clear to us now."

"See that it remains clear," Victor added smoothly, his tone leaving no room for misinterpretation. "We have no patience for political theater. Deliver the final documents to our legal division by midnight."

"It shall be done, Mr. Vance."
