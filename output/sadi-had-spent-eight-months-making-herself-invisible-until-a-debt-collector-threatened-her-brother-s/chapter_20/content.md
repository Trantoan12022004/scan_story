By sunrise, the political landscape of the East Coast had shifted decisively. Senator Vance’s abrupt resignation sent shockwaves through Washington, temporarily paralyzing the regulatory committees that had been primed to target Castello Holdings. In his place, a newly elected, quietly cooperative junior senator—backed entirely by our institutional shell companies—stepped into the committee leadership role, instantly shelving the proposed audit indefinitely.

I stood before the floor-to-ceiling windows of our Manhattan corporate headquarters, looking down at the bustling streets of the financial district as morning traffic hummed far below. The platinum signet ring and diamond band on my finger caught the sharp, brilliant sunlight.

The door to my executive office opened silently, and Victor walked in, holding a steaming cup of dark roast coffee and a fresh intelligence briefing.

"Washington is officially neutralized," Victor announced, handing me the coffee before stepping up beside me to look out over the city. "The new committee chair has already formally dismissed the inquiry. Our maritime logistics pipelines are entirely secure, and the European syndicates are currently tearing themselves apart trying to figure out who leaked Senator Vance's financial records."

I took a slow sip of the coffee, feeling the rich warmth spread through my chest. "They'll never find the leak, Victor, because there was no leak. We simply rewrote the rules of the game while they were still arguing over the old board."

Victor turned his head, looking down at me with an expression of profound, unshakeable adoration. "You know, when my uncle ran this family, he believed that fear was the only tool that mattered. He thought you had to burn cities down to keep people in line."

"Your uncle was a brute with a crown," I replied smoothly, meeting his stormy gray eyes. "Fear makes enemies desperate. Control makes them obedient. We didn't just conquer the underworld, Victor—we institutionalized it."

"And what is our next objective, Empress?" Victor asked with a soft, dark laugh, wrapping his arm around my waist. "Are there any other kingdoms left to conquer?"

"No other kingdoms," I said, looking out toward the endless horizon where the Manhattan skyline met the bright morning sky. "Just maintenance, growth, and absolute dominion."
