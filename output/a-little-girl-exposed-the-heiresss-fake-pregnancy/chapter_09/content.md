PART 9 — THE MAN WHO FINALLY CHOSE

Avery looked at Liam lying on the floor.

Blood pooled beneath his shoulder.

His eyes were open.

Barely.

Conrad tightened his grip around her arm.

"Sign the document."

"Let Leo go."

"You are not negotiating anymore."

Avery slowly reached toward the table.

Conrad watched her hand.

That was his mistake.

She slammed her heel onto his foot and drove her elbow backward into his chest.

Conrad stumbled.

The gun moved away from her body.

Avery grabbed his wrist.

A second shot exploded into the ceiling.

Then every light in the building went black.

Julian.

Emergency strobes activated.

Red.

Darkness.

Red.

Darkness.

Federal agents breached both ends of the corridor.

"FBI!"

"DROP THE WEAPON!"

Conrad fired toward the sound.

Glass shattered.

Avery threw herself toward Leo's room.

The security guard abandoned the child and ran.

Agents tackled him near the stairwell.

Conrad turned toward a service elevator.

He was going to escape.

Then something caught his ankle.

Liam.

Still bleeding.

Still half-conscious.

His hand locked around Conrad's leg.

Conrad kicked him in the face.

Liam didn't release him.

Another kick.

Still nothing.

"You stupid bastard!" Conrad shouted.

Liam looked up.

Blood ran from his mouth.

"For once..."

He tightened his grip.

"...I agree."

Federal agents reached Conrad.

They slammed him onto the floor.

The gun slid away.

Handcuffs closed around his wrists.

For the first time in almost forty years, Conrad Vale had no boardroom, politician, banker or Sterling family member capable of protecting him.

Avery didn't watch.

She broke through the damaged glass door.

"Leo!"

He ran straight into her.

She dropped to her knees.

"Mom!"

"I've got you."

"They said you weren't coming."

"I will always come."

"Always?"

Avery held him tighter.

"Always."

Over his shoulder, she saw paramedics surrounding Liam.

He was conscious.

Their eyes met.

Liam didn't call her name.

Didn't ask to see Leo.

Didn't remind anyone that he had stopped Conrad.

He simply allowed the paramedics to carry him away.

And somehow...

that mattered more to Avery than another thousand apologies.

---

The scandal destroyed what remained of old Sterling Global.

Conrad's arrest opened investigations across six countries.

Federal prosecutors froze dozens of accounts.

Four senior executives resigned within twenty-four hours.

Two were arrested before the week ended.

Investigators uncovered fourteen years of bribery, pension theft, insider manipulation and illegal offshore transfers.

Then Liam Sterling made the decision his lawyers begged him not to make.

From his hospital bed, with a bullet wound through his shoulder, he authorized the public release of every internal audit connected to Conrad's network.

Including records that made Liam himself look negligent.

Including emails proving how often he had chosen his mother's version of events over Avery's.

Including messages Victoria had sent after Avery's disappearance.

One read:

**Do not ask questions that can only hurt you. Avery and the child are gone. Accept it.**

Liam had replied:

**I understand.**

Those two words became front-page news.

Avery watched the press conference alone.

A reporter shouted:

"Mr. Sterling, are you stepping down because you expect criminal charges?"

"No."

"Then why resign?"

Liam looked directly into the cameras.

"Because a man who repeatedly accepts convenient lies should not control the livelihoods of forty thousand people."

Another reporter shouted:

"Do you expect Avery Vance to forgive you?"

"No."

"Do you want her back?"

Liam went silent.

Then:

"What I want stopped being important the day I made her suffering about my pride."

Avery turned off the television.

Not because she hated what he said.

Because she believed him.

And that was more complicated.

---

Three weeks later, Victoria agreed to cooperate fully.

She admitted falsifying medical records.

Bribing hospital employees.

Creating Avery's fake death.

Convincing Liam that his child had died.

Concealing Conrad's involvement.

And helping bury evidence surrounding Arthur Sterling's death.

Then federal investigators found something no one expected.

Arthur's private attorney requested a meeting with Avery.

He arrived carrying a sealed leather folder.

"This document could not be released while Victoria or Conrad retained control of Sterling Global."

Avery opened it.

Arthur had amended his personal estate six months before his death.

She read the first page.

Then stopped.

"This can't be right."

"It is."

Arthur had controlled another eighteen percent of Sterling Global through a separate private vehicle.

The shares were not left to Liam.

Not to Victoria.

Not even to Leo.

They were left to Avery.

But only under two conditions.

She had to be alive.

And investigators had to establish that members of the Sterling family had fraudulently interfered with her marriage and legal identity.

Both conditions were now satisfied.

Combined with Leo's trust...

Avery effectively controlled forty-nine percent of Sterling Global.

Nearly half of the empire Victoria had once told her she was unworthy to enter.

Inside the folder was a handwritten letter.

Avery recognized Arthur's handwriting.

**Avery,**

**They will tell you blood makes someone a Sterling.**

**It does not.**

**Character does.**

**I spent too much of my life building something powerful and not enough time asking whether that power deserved to survive.**

**If my family destroys itself, do not save the dynasty.**

**Save what is worth saving.**

Avery read the final sentence twice.

Julian stood across from her.

"What are you going to do?"

Avery looked toward the Manhattan skyline.

A year earlier, Victoria had tried to erase her from the Sterling family.

Now Avery had enough power to rule it.

Slowly, she closed Arthur's letter.

"I'm going to do exactly what he asked."

Julian raised an eyebrow.

"Save the company?"

Avery shook her head.

"Kill the dynasty."
