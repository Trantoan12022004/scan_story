PART 7 — THE KING BEHIND THE QUEEN

The SUV blew through a red light.

A horn screamed from the intersection.

Leo buried his face against Avery.

"What's happening?"

Avery kept her voice steady.

"We're getting out of the car."

Julian pulled at the rear door.

Locked.

He kicked the emergency release panel.

Nothing.

"Sterling armored transport."

Avery glared at him.

"Designed to protect passengers?"

"And keep them inside during an attack."

"Fantastic."

The two SUVs behind them moved closer.

One pulled alongside.

Tinted windows.

No license plate.

Julian looked toward the driver.

"Conrad's people."

"How do you know?"

"Because federal agents don't remove their plates before kidnapping children."

The vehicle suddenly turned toward the Hudson.

Avery understood.

They weren't being followed.

They were being delivered.

Then headlights appeared from a side street.

A silver Bentley accelerated directly toward them.

Julian's eyes widened.

"What the hell—"

The Bentley slammed into the rear quarter panel.

Metal screamed.

Their SUV spun across two lanes.

Leo cried out.

Avery wrapped herself around him.

The vehicle hit a concrete barrier.

The rear window shattered.

Airbags exploded in front.

Julian kicked the damaged door.

Once.

Twice.

It burst open.

"Move!"

Avery grabbed Leo and climbed into the rain.

The silver Bentley stopped sideways across the street.

A man stepped out.

Avery froze.

Liam.

His tuxedo jacket was gone.

Blood ran from a cut above his eyebrow.

Rain soaked his white shirt.

"You followed me?"

"I saw Conrad's security chief leave the estate three minutes after you."

Avery's expression hardened.

"Stay away from Leo."

Liam stopped instantly.

He looked at the boy.

His son.

Every emotion in his face seemed to collapse at once.

But he didn't move closer.

"I understand."

That surprised her.

Julian looked behind them.

"We don't have time for this."

The first black SUV had stopped.

Men were getting out.

Liam pointed toward an approaching taxi.

"Go."

Avery stared at him.

"What about you?"

"I'll slow them down."

"You think that fixes anything?"

"No."

His answer came without hesitation.

"Nothing I do tonight fixes what I did to you."

For the first time, Liam sounded like a man who understood the difference between apology and consequence.

Avery didn't answer.

Julian pulled her toward the taxi.

They disappeared into traffic.

Liam remained standing in the rain.

And for the first time since discovering that Leo existed...

he did not follow.

---

Three hours later, Avery sat inside a federal safe apartment in Queens.

Leo was asleep in the next room.

Two federal agents guarded the hallway.

Julian was still examining Arthur's files when Avery's secure phone rang.

Unknown number.

She answered.

"Avery."

Victoria.

Avery almost laughed.

"Federal detention allows personal calls now?"

"Don't waste time."

"You seem less confident without your emeralds."

Victoria ignored the insult.

"Did Arthur's protocol activate?"

Avery's smile vanished.

"So you knew."

"Of course I knew."

"Then you knew about Leo's shares."

"Yes."

Everything suddenly clicked.

Avery stood.

"That's why you wanted him gone."

Victoria remained silent.

"You didn't just hate me because I wasn't born into your social class."

"No."

"You needed my child erased because Arthur changed the company."

"Partly."

Avery's hand tightened around the phone.

"Partly?"

Victoria exhaled.

"Conrad knew about the trust before I did."

Avery looked toward Julian.

"He told you to get rid of me."

"No."

Victoria's voice became colder.

"I wanted you gone."

At least she wasn't pretending.

"But I wanted you alive."

Avery laughed bitterly.

"What a comforting distinction."

"I arranged for you to disappear."

"You arranged a forged death certificate."

"Yes."

"You told Liam our child died."

"Yes."

"And the men who later tried to kill us?"

Silence.

Avery's chest tightened.

"Victoria."

"Conrad."

There it was.

The missing piece.

Victoria had created the lie.

Conrad had turned it into attempted murder.

"Why?"

"Because Arthur discovered what Conrad had been doing."

"What was he doing?"

"Stealing."

Avery almost laughed.

"From Sterling Global?"

"From everything."

For more than a decade, Conrad had apparently used shell corporations, pension accounts, acquisition funds and overseas subsidiaries to move billions into private holdings.

Arthur discovered the pattern shortly before Avery became pregnant.

He intended to remove Conrad.

Then Arthur created Leo's trust.

If a legitimate Sterling grandson existed, Conrad could eventually lose control of the company entirely.

"And Arthur died," Avery said.

Victoria went quiet.

The silence answered before she did.

Avery felt sick.

"It wasn't a stroke."

"No."

"Who killed him?"

"Conrad's private physician changed his medication."

Avery closed her eyes.

Arthur Sterling had been murdered.

"And you knew."

"I found out afterward."

"You covered it up."

Victoria's voice cracked.

"I thought protecting Conrad protected Liam."

Avery almost couldn't believe what she was hearing.

"You destroyed your son while claiming you were protecting him."

Another silence.

This one was different.

Smaller.

Almost human.

Then Victoria whispered:

"I know."

Avery had never heard her say those words before.

Before she could respond, Victoria continued.

"Conrad will come for Leo."

"We know."

"He won't kill him immediately."

Avery froze.

"Why?"

"He can't control Arthur's trust without the guardian authorization."

Avery's mouth went dry.

"My signature."

"Yes."

"And if I refuse?"

Victoria's voice fell almost to a whisper.

"He'll force you."

The call disconnected.

Avery stared at the phone.

Then a sound came from Leo's room.

A window.

Julian looked up.

Avery ran.

The bed was empty.

The bathroom window stood open.

The curtain moved in the night air.

Leo's stuffed dinosaur lay on the pillow.

Underneath it was a note.

Avery picked it up.

Four lines.

**MIDNIGHT.**

**HUDSON BIOTECH CAMPUS.**

**COME ALONE AND SIGN THE TRUST TRANSFER.**

And beneath them:

**IF LIAM STERLING CONTACTS THE FBI, HIS SON DIES.**

For the first time that night...

Avery screamed.
