PART 6 — THE DEAD MAN'S FINAL WARNING

The black SUV pulled away from the Sterling Estate just as the rain began falling harder over Manhattan.

Avery sat in the back seat with Leo pressed against her side.

For several blocks, neither of them spoke.

Outside, camera flashes exploded behind the iron gates of the estate. News vans were already blocking half the street. Reporters shouted questions nobody inside the vehicle could hear.

Victoria Sterling had been arrested.

The Sterling Foundation Gala had collapsed into scandal.

And somewhere inside that enormous glass mansion, Liam Sterling was probably still trying to understand how five years of his life had been built on a lie.

Avery stared through the rain-covered window.

She should have felt victorious.

For five years, she had imagined this night.

She had imagined Victoria humiliated.

She had imagined Liam finally realizing that his mother had manipulated him.

She had imagined herself walking away untouched.

But revenge did not feel the way she had expected.

It felt quiet.

Heavy.

Almost empty.

Leo shifted beside her.

"Mom?"

Avery turned immediately.

"I'm here."

The little boy hesitated.

His gray eyes looked almost painfully familiar now.

"That man back there..."

Avery knew exactly which man he meant.

"Yes?"

"Is he really my dad?"

The question pierced straight through every layer of armor she had built.

Avery didn't lie.

"Yes."

Leo looked down at his hands.

"Did he know about me?"

"No."

"Why?"

Avery glanced toward Julian Thorne, who sat across from them.

For once, Julian had no clever answer.

Avery touched Leo's cheek.

"Because some adults told terrible lies."

Leo thought about that.

"Did Dad tell the lies?"

Avery inhaled slowly.

"Not that one."

It was the fairest answer she could give.

Leo leaned against her again.

Avery kissed his hair.

"We'll talk about everything when you're older."

"Okay."

Several minutes passed.

Then Julian's encrypted phone vibrated.

He looked at the screen.

His expression changed.

Avery noticed immediately.

"What?"

Julian didn't answer.

He opened his laptop.

A security alert flashed across the screen.

**STERLING CONTINUITY PROTOCOL ACTIVATED**

Avery frowned.

"What is that?"

"I have no idea."

"You've been inside Sterling's systems for almost a year."

"Exactly."

Julian's fingers moved quickly across the keyboard.

"This isn't corporate infrastructure."

"What is it?"

"Private architecture."

Another password layer opened.

Then another.

Finally, a document appeared.

**ACTIVATION CONDITION ONE: ARREST OR INCAPACITATION OF VICTORIA E. STERLING**

Below it:

**ACTIVATION CONDITION TWO: VERIFIED SURVIVAL OF AVERY STERLING**

Then:

**ACTIVATION CONDITION THREE: BIOLOGICAL CONFIRMATION OF MALE DESCENDANT**

Avery's blood went cold.

"Leo."

Julian didn't answer.

He didn't need to.

A new file automatically unlocked.

**LEO ARTHUR STERLING**

**CONTINGENT BENEFICIARY**

Avery leaned forward.

"Beneficiary of what?"

Julian opened the document.

His eyes widened.

"Thirty-one percent."

"Of what?"

He looked at her.

"Sterling Global."

Avery stared at him.

Sterling Global wasn't just a company.

It owned banks.

Real estate.

Transportation infrastructure.

Medical holdings.

Private equity firms.

Technology investments.

Its total valuation stretched deep into the tens of billions.

"That isn't possible."

"It is."

"Victoria controls the family shares."

"Not these."

Julian scrolled.

"Arthur created a private voting trust seven years ago."

Arthur Sterling.

Liam's father.

The only member of that family who had ever treated Avery as more than an inconvenient outsider.

Arthur had died four years earlier.

Official cause: stroke.

A year after Avery's supposed death.

"Why would Arthur give Leo thirty-one percent?"

Julian's expression darkened.

"I don't think he was giving Leo money."

"What was he doing?"

"Building protection."

Another encrypted file opened automatically.

A video.

Date stamped three days before Arthur Sterling died.

Avery stopped breathing.

Arthur appeared on screen.

He was sitting inside his private library.

Thin.

Exhausted.

Terrified.

"Avery," he began.

Her hand moved to her mouth.

"If you are watching this, then I failed to stop something before it reached you."

Avery leaned closer.

Arthur continued.

"I discovered the truth about your disappearance too late."

Her eyes filled.

"Liam believes you and the baby died. Victoria made certain of that."

Avery's jaw tightened.

"But Victoria is not the person you should fear most."

Julian froze.

Arthur reached toward the camera and held up a photograph.

A silver-haired man stood beside him.

Avery recognized him immediately.

Conrad Vale.

Chairman of Sterling Global.

Arthur's closest friend for nearly forty years.

Liam's godfather.

A man who had hugged Avery at her wedding.

Arthur's voice became quieter.

"Conrad discovered the trust."

Avery felt something shift inside her.

"He knows that if your child survived, the balance of power inside Sterling Global changes permanently."

Arthur looked directly into the camera.

"I made mistakes building this company."

"I trusted men who should never have been trusted."

"And I fear Conrad has already killed to protect what he believes belongs to him."

Julian whispered:

"Jesus Christ."

Arthur continued.

"If Leo survived, Conrad will come for him."

Avery instinctively pulled Leo closer.

"Do not trust the board."

"Do not trust Sterling corporate security."

"And until you know exactly who helped Conrad..."

Arthur paused.

"...do not even trust Liam."

The video ended.

Silence filled the SUV.

Avery stared at the black screen.

Then she noticed something outside.

They were no longer heading toward her penthouse.

She looked through the windshield.

"Julian."

He looked up.

"This isn't our route."

Julian checked the navigation system.

His face hardened.

"No."

Avery leaned toward the privacy partition.

"Driver?"

No response.

Julian pulled his phone out.

"No signal."

"Jammer?"

"Most likely."

The vehicle accelerated.

Leo looked up.

"Mom?"

Avery wrapped both arms around him.

Behind them, two black SUVs appeared through the rain.

Julian reached beneath his jacket.

"Someone knows we opened Arthur's file."

Avery stared through the rear windshield.

The vehicles were closing fast.

Victoria Sterling was in federal custody.

But suddenly Avery understood something terrifying.

Victoria had never been the queen.

She had only been guarding the throne.

And the man sitting behind it had just discovered that Leo Sterling was still alive.
