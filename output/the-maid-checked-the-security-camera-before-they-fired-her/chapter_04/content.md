Richard wrapped his dry suit jacket tightly around his shivering young crying son.

He stood up slowly to face Elena with pure furious anger in his eyes.

Richard:How could you let my helpless boy fall into the deep water?

Elena:Sir, please listen to me for just one short moment!

Victoria:She is lying to save her own low job, Richard!

Victoria stood close behind her husband with a triumphant smug smile on face.

She pretended to wipe away fake tears with a soft small silk handkerchief.

Victoria:I saw her push him with my very own eyes from terrace!

Leo:No, dad, Elena saved me from deep water!

Richard:Hush now, Leo, you are too young to understand this!

Richard was so overwhelmed by panic that he refused to hear his son.

He stepped closer to Elena and spoke in a deep menacing low roar tone.

Richard:You are fired from this household immediately without any pay today!

Elena:Sir, you are making a massive mistake right now!

Victoria:Call the local police department to arrest her right now, dear!

Elena took a deep breath to keep her trembling voice steady and clear now.

She refused to back down in the face of such absolute wicked cruelty.

She raised her shaking hand and pointed upward toward the high mansion roof edge.

Mounted right on the corner pillar was a sleek modern security camera lens shining.

Elena:Check the CCTV footage right now, sir!

Richard:What did you just say to me?

Elena:Look at the camera recordings before you make your final choice!

Victoria face turned pale white like paper under her heavy golden bronzer makeup.

She had completely forgotten about the newly installed outdoor digital security camera system.

Victoria:We do not need to waste time looking at silly recorded videos!

Elena:If you have nothing to hide, then let him check the video screen!

Richard looked up at the high security camera mounted under the white roof tile.

He saw the small blue light blinking on the high resolution digital camera lens.

His logical business mind began to overcome his sudden blind rage and fear now.

He pulled his expensive dark smartphone out of his inner suit jacket pocket quickly.

Richard:I have live stream access to all cameras on my phone.

Victoria:Richard, darling, please just throw this crazy maid off our property!

Richard:Be quiet for one second, Victoria, let me open the app!

Elena stood tall and still despite the dripping cold pool water on her dress.

She knew the truth was recorded on that tiny digital memory card above them.

Victoria stepped back slightly, looking for a fast way out of the patio.

The heavy quiet air grew extremely silent as Richard opened the live security camera log.
