## Chapter 1 The Silent Maid At Grand Manor

The bright sun shone brightly over the huge estate in sunny California.

Elena adjusted her clean white apron while holding a heavy silver tray.

She had worked at the mansion for nearly three full years now.

Her daily job was to keep the vast home spotless and clean.

She always worked hard without complaining about her long tiring daily shifts.

The owner of the home was a wealthy businessman named Richard Vance.

Richard lost his beloved wife four years ago after a long illness.

He lived in the house with his eight year old son named Leo.

Leo was a gentle child who spent most of his days drawing quiet pictures.

He missed his late mother deeply every single day of his life.

Everything changed when Richard married a new woman named Victoria six months ago.

Victoria was a former model who loved high fashion and expensive glittering jewelry.

She wore a sparkling blue diamond dress almost every afternoon on the terrace.

Victoria did not like young Leo playing near her expensive outdoor modern furniture.

She often shouted at the poor boy whenever Richard left for his office downtown.

Elena noticed how cruel Victoria was when the master was not around home.

She always tried her best to protect little Leo from his cold stepmother.

Elena:Do you want some cold lemonade, Leo?

Leo:Yes please, Elena.

Victoria:Stop spoiling that child right now!

Elena lowered her eyes politely and backed away toward the main kitchen doors.

She knew her place in the mansion was to remain completely silent always.

Victoria swept past her while making a loud disgusted noise with her throat.

The atmosphere inside the luxury home had grown tense over the past month.

Victoria wanted full control over the massive family fortune and estate trust funds.

She saw young Leo as a major obstacle in her selfish financial plans.

Elena kept a close eye on the young boy whenever he played outside.

She promised herself that she would keep the innocent child completely safe always.

The afternoon heat was rising fast across the quiet outdoor swimming pool patio.

Elena carried a basket of clean folded towels toward the sunny pool area.

Suddenly she heard a loud angry voice coming from the upper roof terrace.

She stopped near the tall glass doors to listen carefully to the heated sound.

Victoria was shouting at little Leo on the high balcony overlooking the pool.

Victoria:Give me that photo album right now, boy!

Leo:No, this belonged to my real mother!

Victoria:I do not care about your old memories!

Elena gasped as she watched the dramatic argument unfold from down below on patio.

Victoria grabbed the precious photo album and threw it over the glass railing.

The precious book landed near the dangerous edge of the deep blue water pool.

Leo ran down the marble steps crying tears of anger and true heart pain.

Elena dropped her towel basket and moved closer to keep a watching eye.

She knew that Victoria was pushing the poor young boy way too far today.

A storm was brewing over the wealthy family that nobody could stop now.

## Chapter 2 The Incident By The Water

Little Leo reached the edge of the pool to grab his mother photo book.

The smooth marble floor near the water was very slippery from the splash jets.

Victoria walked down the wide patio steps wearing her heavy sparkling diamond dress.

She stood right behind the crying boy with a cold look on her face.

Victoria:Get out of my sight right now!

She reached out her hand aggressively toward the frightened boy near the water.

Leo startled backwards and lost his footing on the slick wet marble tile edge.

He fell straight backward into the deep sparkling water with a loud splashing sound.

The cold pool water immediately swallowed the helpless young boy whole down under.

Victoria watched him struggle without moving a single finger to offer help at all.

She turned around slowly to walk away like nothing bad had just happened there.

From across the wide patio, Elena saw the entire terrible scene happen clearly.

Her heart stopped beating for a brief second in total terror and shock.

She dropped everything in her hands and sprinted full speed across the deck.

Elena:Hold on tight, I have got you!

Her rapid footsteps echoed loudly off the wet pristine white marble patio floor.

She did not hesitate for a single second as she approached the pool edge.

Elena dove headfirst into the cool blue water with an explosive heavy splash sound.

She swam quickly toward the struggling child at the bottom of the deep end.

She wrapped her strong arms securely around the shivering boy under the surface.

Using all her strength, she kicked her legs and kicked up to the surface.

They broke through the water surface as Leo gasped for cold fresh morning air.

Elena pulled him safely up onto the warm stone edge of the swimming pool.

Elena:You are safe now, my sweet boy.

Leo:Thank you, Elena, I was so scared!

The child coughed up water while clutching Elena wet maid uniform tightly in fear.

Just then, the tall glass patio doors slammed open with a loud booming sound.

Richard had just returned home early from his executive business meeting in town.

He heard the loud splashing noise and ran outside onto the marble patio deck.

His eyes widened in shock as he saw his soaking wet shivering son lying there.

Richard:Leo, my son, what on earth happened here?

Victoria:Richard, thank goodness you are finally home!

Victoria rushed out from behind a tall potted plant near the main patio entrance.

She pointed her long manicured finger directly at the soaking wet young maid servant.

Victoria:That careless maid pushed your dear little boy into the deep pool!

Richard face turned red with instant anger as he stared at the brave maid.

He rushed over to comfort his wet son while glaring fiercely at poor Elena.

Elena stood up quickly, dripping water everywhere on the clean bright stone patio floor.

She could not believe the wicked lie coming out of the stepmother red lips.

Elena:That is completely untrue, sir, I swear it!

Richard:Silence right now, you reckless incompetent woman!

The tension in the bright afternoon sunlight was thick enough to cut with knife.

Elena knew she was in deep trouble if she could not prove truth right now.

## Chapter 3 A False Accusation Made

Richard wrapped his dry suit jacket tightly around his shivering young crying son.

He stood up slowly to face Elena with pure furious anger in his eyes.

Richard:How could you let my helpless boy fall into the deep water?

Elena:Sir, please listen to me for just one short moment!

Victoria:She is lying to save her own low job, Richard!

Victoria stood close behind her husband with a triumphant smug smile on face.

She pretended to wipe away fake tears with a soft small silk handkerchief.

Victoria:I saw her push him with my very own eyes from terrace!

Leo:No, dad, Elena saved me from deep water!

Richard:Hush now, Leo, you are too young to understand this!

Richard was so overwhelmed by panic that he refused to hear his son.

He stepped closer to Elena and spoke in a deep menacing low roar tone.

Richard:You are fired from this household immediately without any pay today!

Elena:Sir, you are making a massive mistake right now!

Victoria:Call the local police department to arrest her right now, dear!

Elena took a deep breath to keep her trembling voice steady and clear now.

She refused to back down in the face of such absolute wicked cruelty.

She raised her shaking hand and pointed upward toward the high mansion roof edge.

Mounted right on the corner pillar was a sleek modern security camera lens shining.

Elena:Check the CCTV footage right now, sir!

Richard:What did you just say to me?

Elena:Look at the camera recordings before you make your final choice!

Victoria face turned pale white like paper under her heavy golden bronzer makeup.

She had completely forgotten about the newly installed outdoor digital security camera system.

Victoria:We do not need to waste time looking at silly recorded videos!

Elena:If you have nothing to hide, then let him check the video screen!

Richard looked up at the high security camera mounted under the white roof tile.

He saw the small blue light blinking on the high resolution digital camera lens.

His logical business mind began to overcome his sudden blind rage and fear now.

He pulled his expensive dark smartphone out of his inner suit jacket pocket quickly.

Richard:I have live stream access to all cameras on my phone.

Victoria:Richard, darling, please just throw this crazy maid off our property!

Richard:Be quiet for one second, Victoria, let me open the app!

Elena stood tall and still despite the dripping cold pool water on her dress.

She knew the truth was recorded on that tiny digital memory card above them.

Victoria stepped back slightly, looking for a fast way out of the patio.

The heavy quiet air grew extremely silent as Richard opened the live security camera log.

## Chapter 4 The Unfiltered Truth Revealed

Richard fingers moved quickly across the glass screen of his shiny black phone.

He tapped on the surveillance app icon and selected the high definition outdoor camera angle.

The screen loaded a clear digital video recording from five minutes ago today.

A digital chime rang softly from the phone speakers as the video started playing.

Richard watched the video screen with deep focused concentration on his sharp face.

The recording clearly showed Victoria standing over young Leo on the high terrace edge.

It showed her snatching the precious late mother photo album from the poor child hands.

It showed her throw the album down into the pool area with absolute cruelty.

Then the video camera captured the next terrifying moment on the lower outdoor patio.

Victoria stepped right up behind little Leo and aggressively startled the frightened boy.

She backed him into the slippery water edge until he fell backward straight in.

The video clearly showed her standing there cold while the boy struggled underwater alone.

Then it showed Elena sprinting full speed across patio to dive into pool bravely.

It showed Elena saving his son life while Victoria watched without helping at all.

Richard stood completely frozen in absolute shock as the video finished playing through.

A deep heavy silence fell over the bright outdoor swimming pool marble patio area.

His face contorted into pure intense rage as the horrible dark truth sank in.

Veins bulged on his forehead and his heavy jaw was clenched tight with fury.

He raised his dark eyes slowly from the phone screen to look at Victoria.

Victoria backed away slowly until her back hit the cold outdoor stone wall behind.

Richard:You absolute monster, how could you do this to my son?

Victoria:Richard, please, I can explain everything if you just listen!

Richard:Do not say another word to me or my child ever again!

Elena:I tried to tell you the truth, Mr. Vance.

Richard turned to look at Elena with deep shame visible in his brown eyes.

He realized how terribly wrong he had been about his faithful young house servant.

Richard:Elena, I am so deeply sorry for speaking to you like that.

Elena:You were just protecting your son, sir, I understand completely.

Leo:See dad, I told you Elena was the good one here!

Richard knelt down and hugged his soaking wet son tighter than ever before today.

He looked up at his cold second wife with lethal calculating fury in eyes.

The truth was out now, and there was no going back for anyone here.

## Chapter 5 Justice Served At Last

Richard stood up tall and walked heavily toward his terrified wicked stepmother wife.

His heavy black dress shoes made loud firm footsteps on the wet marble patio floor.

Richard:You are going to pay for every single thing you did today!

Victoria:What are you going to do to me, Richard?

Richard:I am calling my divorce lawyer and the police force right now!

He pulled out his phone again and dialed the local emergency police service number.

He reported the child endangerment act recorded clearly on his private home security cameras.

Victoria tried to run inside to grab her expensive diamond ring collection boxes upstairs.

Richard stepped firmly in front of the tall glass sliding door to block her way.

Richard:You will not take one single item from this home today!

Within ten short minutes, the loud sound of police sirens echoed down the road.

Two uniformed police officers walked onto the bright sunlit poolside patio deck area quickly.

Richard showed the lead officer the clear high definition video proof on his phone screen.

The officers immediately placed cold heavy steel handcuffs around Victoria thin manicured wrists.

Victoria:You cannot arrest me, this mansion is my official home too!

Officer:You have the right to remain completely silent right now, ma'am.

They led the screaming furious woman away down the long paved stone drive driveway.

She would face serious criminal charges for child endangerment and fraudulent false reports.

Richard turned back to face Elena who was helping Leo dry off with towels.

He walked over to the brave house maid with immense deep gratitude in heart.

Richard:You saved my son life today and saved our home from evil.

Elena:I was just doing my duty to protect this family, sir.

Richard:You are far more than a maid to us from this day forward.

He offered her a permanent position as the head manager of his entire estate.

He doubled her monthly salary and gave her a generous legal trust fund bonus.

Leo wrapped his small warm arms around Elena waist in a loving hug frame.

Leo:Please never leave us, Elena, you are family now!

Elena:I am not going anywhere, my dear brave little boy.

The dark shadow over the grand mansion had finally cleared under the afternoon sun.

Honesty and courage had brought absolute justice and real peace back home today.
