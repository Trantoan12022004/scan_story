Elena Brooks.

Thirty-six.

Severe pneumonia.

Malnutrition.

Complications from untreated autoimmune disease.

Not dying.

But sick.

Very sick.

Clara had left the hospital after overhearing a social worker mention emergency foster placement if Elena remained unable to care for both children.

Elena had given her Adrian’s name before falling asleep after medication.

Clara panicked.

She found an old bus route on Elena’s phone.

Then a wedding announcement online.

And came.

With Mia.

Alone.

Adrian felt physically sick when he learned that.

An eight-year-old had crossed Chicago with a baby because adults had left her believing she had to solve everything herself.

No.

That ended now.

Hospital social workers became involved.

So did child protective services.

Not because Adrian wanted to seize the girls.

Because safe temporary care needed to be arranged properly.

Elena was still their mother.

Adrian had no verified legal status yet.

He respected that.

Then he walked into Elena’s hospital room.

She was asleep.

For several seconds, he could not move.

Eight years.

He had dreamed of her.

Dreamed she survived.

Dreamed she hated him.

Dreamed she needed help.

Then trained himself to stop.

Now she was twenty feet away.

Older.

Thinner.

Alive.

Her eyes opened.

She saw him.

And started crying.

“Adrian.”

He sat down.

Neither touched the other.

Not yet.

He asked the only safe question.

“Is Clara mine?”

Elena nodded.

“Yes.”

“Do you have proof?”

Her face tightened.

“Yes.”

Good.

No offense at verification.

She understood.

“Where?”

“Storage box.”

“Who has access?”

“Attorney.”

“What attorney?”

“Naomi Fields.”

Adrian knew the name.

A respected family-law attorney who had retired three years earlier.

Good.

Independent.

Then:

“Why didn’t you call me?”

Elena cried harder.

“I did.”

“How many times?”

“Hundreds.”

His chest tightened.

“I never got them.”

“I know.”

That answer hurt more.

“You know?”

“Eventually.”

Then:

“Victoria intercepted things.”

Adrian sat back.

“How?”

Elena explained.

After disappearing, she first lived in Indiana under her own name.

She sent letters through an attorney.

They came back.

She called Adrian’s old office.

His assistant said all personal messages had to go through family security due threats connected to the company.

Who controlled that security transition?

Raymond Langford.

At the time, Raymond was outside counsel advising Mercer Maritime during a succession dispute.

Victoria worked in his office.

Elena did not know Adrian and Victoria would later become involved.

Back then, Victoria was simply the woman answering calls.

Then Elena showed Adrian an old email.

Victoria:

Mr. Mercer does not wish contact. Continued attempts may be treated as harassment.

Adrian stared.

He had never written that.

Never authorized it.

Then another:

Adrian has accepted that Clara is not his child.

He had never known Clara existed.

The lie had been complete.

Not misunderstanding.

Interception.
