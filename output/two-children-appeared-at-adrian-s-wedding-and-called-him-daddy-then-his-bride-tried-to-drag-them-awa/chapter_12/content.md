Elena remained hospitalized.

Temporary care was shared through court-approved arrangements.

Clara spent one weekend at Adrian’s home.

She arrived with one duffel bag.

Not because she owned nothing.

Because moving a child through crisis should be simple.

Adrian showed her the guest room.

She asked:

“Is this mine?”

“For the weekend.”

That distinction helped.

Then:

“Can I lock the door?”

His chest tightened.

“Yes.”

“Will anyone come in?”

“Not without knocking unless there’s an emergency.”

She nodded.

Boundary.

Small.

Important.

Then she asked:

“Do you have cereal?”

Adrian laughed.

“About twelve kinds.”

She looked impressed for the first time.

Not by the mansion.

Cereal.

Good.
