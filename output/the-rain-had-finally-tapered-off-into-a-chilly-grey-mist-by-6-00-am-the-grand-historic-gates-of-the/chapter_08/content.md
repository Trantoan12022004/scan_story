Julian Vance stood in the airy drawing room, wearing a tailored navy blazer and brown leather boots. He was tall, with warm brown eyes, dark hair streaked with silver at the temples, and a smile that reached all the way to his eyes.

"Claire," Julian said softly, stepping forward and offering a warm, genuine embrace. "It’s good to see you looking so well."

"Julian," Claire smiled, stepping back to look at him. "Thank you for coming all the way from Chicago."

Julian unrolled a large set of architectural blueprints across the central marble table. "I heard about everything that happened over the past two months. You’ve been through a storm, Claire."

"The storm cleared away the rotten wood, Julian," Claire said thoughtfully, looking down at the designs. "Now it's time to build something strong."

Julian pointing to the grand arches and sunlit courtyard of the proposed community center. "This center will provide emergency shelter, legal aid, and financial education for women escaping toxic households or financial abuse. We’ve designed it with open spaces, natural light, and private gardens—a place of sanctuary and empowerment."

Claire traced her fingers over the drawn arches, deeply moved by the care and detail Julian had put into the design.

"It’s perfect, Julian," she whispered, looking up into his eyes. "You captured exactly what I felt when I was standing in that rain two months ago."

Julian looked at her with an intensity that made her breath catch. "Claire, when I worked with you three years ago in London, I saw how much light you brought into every room, despite the heavy burden you were carrying at home. You deserve a life filled with that same light."

Claire blushed slightly, feeling a long-dormant spark reignite within her chest. For seven years, Ethan had made her feel small, broken, and unlovable. But standing beside Julian, she felt seen, valued, and deeply respected.

"Thank you, Julian," Claire said genuinely. "Will you stay for dinner tonight? We have a lot more than blueprints to talk about."

Julian smiled warmly, his hand resting gently over hers on the table. "I would be honored, Claire."
