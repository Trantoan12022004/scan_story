The massive front double doors swung open with a heavy and decisive sound.

Richard Vance stepped into the foyer wearing a tailored black business suit jacket.

He was accompanied by two stern security officers and his head corporate legal counsel.

Richard had taken an earlier flight back to Dallas after reviewing the red bank flags.

He had seen suspicious wire transfers leaving his family trust account all week long.

The moment he stepped across the marble threshold, he saw the shocking scene unfolding.

His daughter was sitting on the wet floor, her white dress soaked and dirty.

Nora was standing over her with an angry, flushed expression on her face.

Richard's eyes filled with immediate rage as he rushed forward across the foyer.

Richard:What on earth is going on inside my home right now?

Nora instantly changed her expression, putting on a fake look of deep worry.

Nora:Oh Richard darling! You arrived home so much earlier than we expected!

She stepped in front of Elena, trying to block Richard's view of the child.

Nora:Elena threw a terrible temper tantrum and spilled water all over the floor.

Nora:I was just trying to teach her basic discipline and order around here.

Richard ignored Nora completely and dropped to his knees right beside his daughter.

He wrapped his large arms around Elena, lifting her gently off the cold tile.

Richard:Are you alright, sweetie? Daddy is right here, I've got you now.

Elena held onto her father's neck tightly, sobbing heavily against his warm suit jacket.

Elena:Dad, she shoved me down and made me scrub floors all day long!

Elena:And she took Mom's diamond ring from your private office safe downstairs!

Nora's face went completely pale as the corporate lawyer pulled out a official folder.

Nora:She is lying, Richard! The child is just making up wild stories to hurt me!

Richard stood up slowly, keeping one protective arm tightly around his young daughter.

His face was hard as stone as he glared down at his trembling wife.

Richard:My daughter does not lie to me, Nora, but your bank records certainly do.

The security guards stepped forward, blocking every single exit route from the foyer.

Nora backed up slowly against the marble wall, realizing her entire plot had failed.
