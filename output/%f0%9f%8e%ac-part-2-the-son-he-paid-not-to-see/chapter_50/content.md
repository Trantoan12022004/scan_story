The older woman turned toward the woman in emerald.

“What did she just say?”

The woman stepped back.

“She’s a child. She doesn’t know what she’s talking about.”

The girl reached into her cardigan pocket.

“I have this.”

She pulled out an old folded photograph.

The older woman took it.

Her daughter stood outside the same mansion years earlier.

Pregnant.

On the back was a date.

And one sentence:

I came home. They told me you never wanted to see me again.

The older woman’s face collapsed.

“I was in Paris that week.”

The woman in emerald lowered her eyes.

The truth began to surface.

Years earlier, the older woman’s daughter had fallen in love with a man the family considered beneath them.

She left.

Then came back pregnant.

But she never made it past the front hall.

The woman in emerald had been there.

Much younger.

Already determined to marry into the family.

She had told the daughter her mother wanted nothing to do with her.

The older woman stared at her.

“You lied to my child?”

The woman’s voice cracked.

“She would have inherited everything.”

Silence.

The little girl tightened her arms around her grandmother.

The older woman looked down.

“Where is your mother now?”

The girl’s lips trembled.

“In the hospital.”

The older woman stopped breathing.

“She’s sick?”

The girl nodded.

“She told me to sell the roses so I could buy a bus ticket home.”

The older woman looked around the ballroom.

Thousands of dollars in flowers.

Champagne.

Diamonds.

And her granddaughter had been trying to sell roses to afford a bus ride.

She stood.

“Get my car.”

The woman in emerald whispered,

“Please…”

The older woman looked at her.

“No.”

Her voice was quiet now.

“You took my daughter from me once.”

Then she took the little girl’s hand.

“You don’t get to take another minute.”

The girl looked up.

“Are we going to see Mom?”

The older woman knelt again.

“Yes.”

Tears filled her eyes.

“And this time, when your mother asks whether I wanted her…”

She held the crescent pendant between them.

“…she’s going to hear the answer from me.”
