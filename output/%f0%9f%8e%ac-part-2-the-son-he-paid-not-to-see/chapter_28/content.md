Daniel slowly stood.

“What did you just say?”

The officer reached into his wallet.

His hands were suddenly trembling too.

He pulled out an old photograph.

A much younger Mary stood outside the same diner beside a man in a police uniform.

Daniel stared at it.

“Who is that?”

The officer looked at him.

“My father.”

Mary’s face drained of color.

“No…”

Daniel turned toward her.

“Mom?”

She sat down hard in the booth.

The officer continued quietly.

“My father said the woman he loved disappeared before he could marry her.”

Mary covered her mouth.

“I didn’t disappear.”

Daniel frowned.

“What happened?”

She looked at the photograph.

“His family told me he had chosen someone else.”

The officer shook his head.

“They told him you left town with another man.”

Silence.

Daniel stared between them.

Then his eyes moved back to the photograph.

The man standing beside Mary had the same eyes.

The same jaw.

Even the same small scar near his eyebrow.

Daniel’s voice dropped.

“Mom…”

She couldn’t look at him.

“Who was my father?”

Mary began crying.

The officer already understood.

He looked at Daniel with shock.

“My father died last year.”

Daniel swallowed.

Then the officer said quietly,

“And according to this photograph…”

He looked at Mary.

“…I think he was your father too.”
