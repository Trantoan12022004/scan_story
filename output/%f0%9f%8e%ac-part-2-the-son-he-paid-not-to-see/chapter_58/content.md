Adrian slowly released the maid.

“Show me.”

His daughter walked toward him.

The phone shook in her hands as she opened the video.

The lobby fell silent.

On the screen, the staircase appeared.

The maid was pushing the stroller carefully.

His wife was beside her.

Then came the moment nobody could explain away.

One champagne-colored heel moved behind the maid’s foot.

Deliberately.

The maid tripped.

Adrian stopped the video.

His face went completely still.

He replayed it.

Once.

Then again.

His daughter whispered,

“She did it on purpose.”

The maid covered her mouth as tears filled her eyes.

Adrian looked at her bruised knees.

Then at the stroller she had nearly broken herself protecting.

Finally, he turned toward his wife.

“You lied to me.”

Her confidence disappeared.

“Adrian, listen…”

“You watched her fall.”

His voice was quiet now.

That frightened her more than shouting would have.

“And then you blamed her.”

“I panicked.”

“No.”

His eyes moved toward the phone.

“You planned it.”

She stepped backward.

Her hands began to shake.

“Please. You don’t understand.”

The maid crawled closer to the stroller and touched the cream blanket with trembling fingers, checking the baby again.

Adrian saw it.

Even after being accused.

Even after being hurt.

She was still worried about his child first.

His face crumpled.

He turned back to his wife.

“You almost destroyed my family.”

The blonde woman’s eyes filled with panic.

“I was trying to protect it.”

“From what?”

Silence.

His daughter looked between them.

“Mom?”

The woman closed her eyes.

And that single word seemed to break something inside her.

A police officer who had been standing near the entrance stepped closer.

“Ma’am, I need you to explain what happened.”

She said nothing.

Adrian stared at her.

“Why did you want her gone?”

The maid slowly looked up.

The blonde woman’s face went pale.

Then the little girl glanced at the maid.

At the baby.

And finally at her father.

“There’s something else on the video.”

Adrian turned.

“What?”

His daughter’s lips trembled.

“Before she tripped her… Mom said something.”

She raised the phone again.

A faint voice played through the speaker.

The blonde woman’s voice.

“You should’ve disappeared the day Adrian found out that baby was his.”

The maid stopped breathing.

Adrian’s face drained of color.

He looked at the stroller.

Then at the maid.

Then at his wife.

“What does that mean?”

The blonde woman began to cry for real.

And suddenly everyone understood.

The fall had never been about getting rid of a maid.

It was about hiding who the baby’s mother really was.
