Lily looked directly at their uncle David.

Emma froze.

“No.”

David had been there through everything.

He had driven Lily to therapy.

Sat beside their mother at every court hearing.

He had even been the one who convinced Emma that the dead man was guilty.

Emma shook her head.

“Lily… don’t.”

Lily started sobbing.

“He told me nobody would believe me.”

David stepped forward.

“She’s confused.”

Lily flinched immediately.

Emma noticed.

That tiny movement told her everything.

She stepped between them.

“Don’t go near her.”

Their mother looked at David.

Her face slowly collapsed.

“What did you do?”

David said nothing.

Lily reached into her coat and pulled out an old phone.

“I recorded him.”

David’s expression changed.

Emma stared at the phone.

“You had proof?”

Lily nodded through tears.

“But he said if I told anyone, he’d hurt Mom.”

Their mother covered her mouth.

Emma looked toward the coffin.

The man they were burying had died hated by everyone.

Accused by his own family.

Never believed.

Emma’s voice broke.

“He died thinking we believed you instead of him.”

Lily whispered,

“I know.”

Emma turned back toward David.

For the first time, he looked afraid.

Then Lily pressed play.

His own voice came through the phone.

And every mourner around the coffin heard the truth.
