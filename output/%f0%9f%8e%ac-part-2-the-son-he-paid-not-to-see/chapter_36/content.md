The mother stared at Luca.

“What is he talking about?”

Luca looked at the newborn.

Then back at her.

“Your son’s father was Daniel Moretti.”

She shook her head.

“No. His name was Daniel Reed.”

“That’s the name he used to protect you.”

Her face went pale.

Luca continued quietly.

“Daniel was my younger brother.”

The man in blue pushed himself upright.

“And our father’s favorite son.”

The mother looked at him.

“Who are you?”

“Vincent Moretti.”

Luca’s expression hardened.

“Our half-brother.”

Security appeared at the far end of the hallway.

But the mother barely noticed.

“Where is Daniel?”

Luca’s eyes changed.

“He died six weeks ago.”

She stopped breathing.

“No.”

“He was coming back to you.”

“You’re lying.”

Luca reached inside his coat and removed a sealed envelope.

Her name was written across it.

In Daniel’s handwriting.

She began crying before she even opened it.

Inside was a letter.

And a photograph.

Daniel stood beside Luca.

On the back, Daniel had written:

If anything happens to me, Luca protects them.

The mother looked at the man in blue.

“Why was he trying to take my baby?”

Luca answered.

“Because Daniel discovered our father changed his will.”

Vincent’s face tightened.

Luca looked toward the newborn.

“Everything Daniel inherited passes to his child.”

The mother finally understood.

“He wasn’t taking my son because he cared about him.”

“No.”

Luca stepped between Vincent and the baby again.

“He needed control of him.”

Security pulled Vincent away.

He shouted down the hallway,

“That child belongs to the Moretti family!”

Luca didn’t even turn around.

He looked at the terrified mother.

Then quietly said,

“He belongs to you.”

Her tears fell harder.

She glanced at the signature on the clipboard.

“Then why did you sign as his guardian?”

Luca’s voice cracked.

“Because Daniel asked me to if he didn’t make it home.”

She looked at her sleeping baby.

Then at the man who had arrived like a stranger.

After a long silence, she moved the blanket slightly.

“His name is Noah.”

Luca’s eyes filled.

“That was Daniel’s middle name.”

She nodded.

“I know.”

For the first time, Luca lost his composure.

And the mother finally understood why the powerful stranger had come running into that hospital:

he wasn’t there to claim her child.

He was there to keep his dead brother’s promise.
