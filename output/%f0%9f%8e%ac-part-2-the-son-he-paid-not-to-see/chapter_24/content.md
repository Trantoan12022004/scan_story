Emily stared at the boy.

“My brother?”

Her mother nodded.

“His name is Noah.”

The boy pulled back slightly.

“You know my name?”

Her face collapsed.

“I gave you that name.”

Noah’s eyes filled.

“The man I stayed with said my mother didn’t want me.”

She shook her head desperately.

“I looked for you for eight years.”

Noah frowned.

“But he said you knew where I was.”

The woman froze.

“Who said that?”

Noah reached inside his torn shirt and pulled out an old photograph.

She took it.

Her hands immediately began shaking.

It showed her holding newborn Noah in a hospital bed.

Beside her stood the doctor who had told her the baby died.

Emily looked at the picture.

“Mom… who is that?”

The woman could barely speak.

“The man who signed your brother’s death certificate.”

Noah looked toward the end of the alley.

His face suddenly went pale.

“He makes me sleep outside when I don’t bring enough money.”

The mother stood.

“Who?”

Noah pointed.

A man had just stepped into the alley.

The woman recognized him instantly.

But it wasn’t the doctor.

It was someone who had sat at her family table for years.

Someone who had comforted her after Noah’s funeral.

Noah grabbed her coat.

“That’s him.”

Her breathing stopped.

Because the man walking toward them…

was Emily’s father.
