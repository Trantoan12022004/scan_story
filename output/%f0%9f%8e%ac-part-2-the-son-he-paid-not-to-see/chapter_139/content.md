The man followed her eyes.

Nobody came through the doors.

But the woman’s fear was real.

He looked back at her.

“What did you do?”

She shook her head.

“Nothing.”

The security monitor still glowed behind them.

The maid stood beside it, wiping tears from her cheeks.

The man held up the necklace.

“You accused an innocent woman because you wanted an insurance claim.”

Silence.

The bronze-dressed woman finally whispered,

“I needed money fast.”

Her husband had trusted her with the family charity accounts while he was overseas.

At first, she borrowed a small amount.

She planned to replace it.

Then another payment came due.

Then another.

Soon, she was moving money from one account to cover another, convincing herself she could fix everything before anyone noticed.

But an audit had been scheduled.

Her husband was returning that night.

She needed a believable loss.

Something valuable.

Something insured.

The necklace was perfect.

If it disappeared during a crowded event, the claim might buy her enough time to replace what was missing.

But she needed someone to blame.

Someone people would believe could have taken it.

The maid.

Young.

Poor.

Easy to dismiss.

The maid stared at her through tears.

“You were going to ruin my life.”

The woman looked away.

The man’s face hardened.

Then he noticed something on the security footage.

He stepped closer to the monitor.

Rewound it.

Watched again.

The woman planting the necklace.

Then, seconds later—

another figure appeared at the far edge of the camera.

A man in a dark suit.

Watching her.

The bronze-dressed woman saw him too.

Her face went white.

The man in olive turned toward her.

“You knew someone saw you.”

She closed her eyes.

That was why she had panicked.

Not because the camera exposed her.

Because the person she feared finding out had already been there.

The maid looked at the screen.

“Who is that?”

The woman didn’t answer.

The man in olive stared at the frozen image.

Then recognized the figure.

Her husband.

He had returned early.

And according to the timestamp—

he had watched her plant the necklace before the accusation ever began.

The bronze-dressed woman started crying.

All this time, she thought she was protecting herself until he came home.

But he had already seen everything.

The man placed the necklace gently on the table.

The maid stood beside him, no longer shaking.

And behind the closed doors at the end of the hallway, footsteps finally began approaching.
