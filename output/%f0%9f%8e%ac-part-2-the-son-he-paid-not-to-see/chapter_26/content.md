Colton stared at the little girl.

She was still clinging to her mother.

“What is she talking about?”

The maid lowered her eyes.

“Nothing.”

The blonde woman laughed nervously.

“Tell him.”

Colton’s voice hardened.

“Tell me what?”

The maid began crying.

Seven years earlier, Colton had loved a woman named Anna.

Then one morning, she disappeared.

No goodbye.

No explanation.

The woman kneeling in front of him looked older now.

Exhausted.

Broken down by years he knew nothing about.

But suddenly he saw it.

“Anna?”

She closed her eyes.

The little girl looked between them.

“You know my mama?”

Colton could barely breathe.

“I thought you left me.”

Anna shook her head.

“I was made to leave.”

“By who?”

Her eyes moved toward the blonde woman.

Colton turned slowly.

The blonde woman backed away.

Anna whispered,

“She found out I was pregnant.”

Colton looked at the child.

His face emptied.

“No…”

Anna’s lips trembled.

“She told me your family would destroy me if I ever came back.”

Colton dropped to one knee.

The little girl stared at him.

He noticed the shape of her eyes.

The small dimple beside her mouth.

The same one his mother had.

His voice broke.

“How old are you?”

“Seven.”

Colton looked at Anna.

She nodded through tears.

“Yes.”

The little girl whispered,

“What?”

Anna pulled her close.

“Sweetheart…”

But Colton was already crying.

He had spent seven years believing Anna abandoned him.

While his own daughter had been growing up somewhere else.

Then he looked at the servant dress the child had been forced to wear.

His expression changed.

“Why is my daughter dressed like this?”

Anna went silent.

The blonde woman suddenly moved toward the door.

Colton blocked her.

“No.”

She froze.

He looked at Anna.

“What else did she do?”

Anna’s hands began shaking.

Then the little girl answered for her.

“She said if Mama ever told you who I was…”

Colton looked down.

The child’s eyes filled.

“…she’d make us disappear again.”
