Before the judge could speak, a broken voice came from the gallery.

“Your Honor… wait.”

Everyone turned.

The woman who had been crying stood on shaking legs.

The teenage boy stared at her.

The judge frowned.

“Who are you?”

She wiped tears from her face.

“Their mother’s sister.”

The teenager went completely still.

“My mom didn’t have a sister.”

The woman’s face collapsed.

“She did.”

She stepped closer.

“She just stopped speaking to me.”

The older boy pulled his brother behind him.

“Why?”

Her lips trembled.

“Because when she needed me most, I chose our father instead of her.”

Silence filled the courtroom.

“I’ve regretted it every day.”

The teenager’s eyes hardened.

“So now you want us?”

She shook her head.

“No.”

That answer surprised everyone.

“I want you to keep your promise.”

The boy stared at her.

She turned toward the judge.

“If he can’t legally do this alone yet…”

Her voice broke.

“Let me help him.”

The lawyer slowly lowered his papers.

The judge looked from the woman to the brothers.

Then back to the teenager.

The younger boy whispered,

“Are they still taking you away?”

The teenager couldn’t answer.

The judge leaned forward.

“No.”

Both boys froze.

The judge’s voice softened.

“Not today.”

The younger boy began crying again, but differently this time.

The teenager covered his face.

The judge looked directly at him.

“You kept your promise when every adult around you was deciding what you couldn’t do.”

The boy lowered his hands.

Then the judge said the words that finally broke him.

“Now let the adults in this room help you keep it.”
