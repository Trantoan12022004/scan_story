The man slowly turned toward the woman.

“What does she mean?”

The woman shook her head.

“She’s upset. She doesn’t know what she’s saying.”

“I do.”

The girl’s voice was stronger now.

The man faced his daughter again.

“Tell me.”

She looked down at her trembling legs.

“I started standing months ago.”

His face collapsed.

“Months?”

She nodded.

“But every time I got better, she made me sit back down.”

The woman stepped forward.

“That’s not true.”

The girl flinched.

Her father immediately moved between them.

“Stay away from her.”

The woman stopped.

The girl reached for her father’s hand.

“She said if you knew I could walk, you wouldn’t need her anymore.”

The man went completely still.

The woman’s composure finally cracked.

“I took care of both of you!”

The girl shook her head.

“You kept me helpless.”

Silence fell across the driveway.

The man’s hand began trembling around his daughter’s.

Then the girl looked toward the house.

“There’s something under my bed.”

His eyes narrowed.

“What?”

“A phone.”

The woman’s face drained of color.

The girl looked directly at her father.

“I recorded her.”

The woman took a sudden step backward.

The man stared at her.

And the girl said the sentence that finally explained everything.

“She wasn’t spraying me because I was dirty.”

Her voice broke.

“She was punishing me because she caught me practicing how to walk.”
