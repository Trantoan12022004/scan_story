The mother stared at Luca.

“My baby’s father was Daniel.”

Luca nodded.

“I know.”

“Then what does your name have to do with him?”

Luca looked away for a moment.

“Daniel Moretti was my younger brother.”

Her lips parted.

“No. Daniel never told me he had a brother.”

“He was trying to keep you away from our family.”

The blue-suited man slowly pushed himself upright as hospital security arrived.

“Don’t believe him.”

Luca turned toward him.

“You’ve said enough, Vincent.”

The mother looked between them.

“You know each other?”

Luca’s voice hardened.

“He’s our half-brother.”

She instinctively held the baby tighter.

Vincent had spent months telling her Daniel had abandoned her.

Then, after the birth, he suddenly appeared demanding the child.

“Where is Daniel?” she whispered.

Luca’s eyes filled for the first time.

“He died three weeks ago.”

Her knees nearly gave out.

“No…”

Luca caught her elbow without touching the baby.

“He was coming back to you.”

She began shaking.

“He left me.”

“No.”

Luca reached inside his coat and handed her a sealed envelope.

Her name was written across it in Daniel’s handwriting.

“He disappeared because Vincent threatened you.”

Vincent’s face went pale.

Luca continued.

“Daniel discovered our father’s will.”

The mother looked down at her newborn.

“What does that have to do with my son?”

Luca swallowed.

“Everything.”

Their father’s estate had one final condition.

If Daniel had a child, that child became the principal heir.

The mother finally understood why Vincent had come to the hospital.

“He didn’t want my baby…”

Luca shook his head.

“He wanted control of what belongs to him.”

Security pulled Vincent to his feet.

He shouted, “That inheritance belongs to our family!”

Luca stepped between him and the mother.

“He is our family.”

Vincent was taken down the corridor.

Only after he disappeared did the mother open Daniel’s letter.

Her tears fell onto the page.

At the bottom was one final sentence:

If I don’t make it back, trust Luca with our son. He’s the only brother I have left.

She looked up at Luca.

“So that signature…”

Luca nodded.

“I’m not trying to replace his father.”

His voice cracked.

“I’m trying to keep the promise his father died believing I would keep.”

The newborn made a tiny sound beneath the blanket.

Luca looked at him but didn’t reach for him.

The mother noticed.

After a long moment, she carefully moved the baby closer.

“You can hold your nephew.”

Luca’s composure finally broke.

And as he took his brother’s son into his arms, the powerful stranger who had terrified everyone in that hallway began to cry.
