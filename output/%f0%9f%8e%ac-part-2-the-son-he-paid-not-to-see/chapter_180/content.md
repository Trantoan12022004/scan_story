The man’s hand stopped inches from hers.

The chapel went deathly quiet.

“Emily?”

Her fingers recoiled from him.

The maid immediately reached into the broken coffin instead.

“I’m here.”

Emily grabbed her hand.

Hard.

The man stared at his wife.

“What are you saying?”

Emily’s breathing was shallow.

Her eyes struggled open.

Then they found him.

And pure fear crossed her face.

“You knew.”

His expression changed.

Only slightly.

But the maid saw it.

Emily tried to push herself higher.

“You knew I was still breathing.”

The man shook his head.

“No. You’re confused.”

The maid looked at him.

Then at Emily.

Emily’s voice cracked.

“He gave me something.”

The man stepped back.

Murmurs spread through the mourners.

Emily’s grip tightened around the maid’s hand.

“I woke up before they closed the coffin.”

Tears slipped toward her temples.

“I tried to move.”

The maid covered her mouth.

Emily stared at her husband.

“You were standing beside me.”

His face went pale.

“No…”

“You leaned over me.”

Her breath caught painfully.

“And I heard you say…”

The man’s lips began trembling.

Emily’s eyes filled with tears.

“‘By tomorrow, everything will finally be mine.’”

The maid slowly turned toward him.

But Emily wasn’t finished.

She looked at the black rose pinned to his suit.

Then whispered the truth that emptied the room of sound.

“He didn’t come here to bury his wife.”

Her shaking hand pointed at him.

“He came to make sure I never woke up.”
