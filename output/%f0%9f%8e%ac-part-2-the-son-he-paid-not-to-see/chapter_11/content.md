“Alive.”

Sarah stared at her.

“No.”

Another violent bang shook the door.

“You’re lying.”

The young woman unlocked her phone with trembling fingers.

“Look.”

She opened a video.

A gray-haired woman sat inside a small room, looking straight into the camera.

Sarah’s knees almost gave out.

“Mom?”

Older now.

Thinner.

But unmistakably her.

The recording continued.

“If Sarah ever sees this, tell her I never abandoned her.”

Sarah covered her mouth.

Her husband whispered,

“I saw the video tonight too.”

Sarah looked at him.

“You knew?”

“For twenty minutes.”

Another bang hit the door.

Her brother shouted from outside,

“SARAH, DON’T LISTEN TO HER!”

Sarah flinched at his voice.

The young woman began crying harder.

“He found out I had the recording.”

Sarah stared at the phone.

“Why would my brother hide my mother from me?”

The woman hesitated.

“Because your mother discovered what he’d been doing with your family’s money.”

Sarah shook her head.

“That doesn’t explain a funeral.”

Her husband looked at the photograph again.

Then zoomed in on the second person beside Sarah’s brother.

His expression changed.

“Sarah…”

“What?”

He turned the phone toward her.

The second person was the doctor who had signed her mother’s death certificate.

Sarah stopped breathing.

The young woman whispered,

“There was never a body in that coffin.”

Sarah looked toward the shaking hotel door.

“My brother arranged all of this?”

“No.”

The woman’s face filled with fear.

“He helped.”

Sarah slowly turned back.

“Helped who?”

The young woman looked at the video of Sarah’s mother.

Then whispered,

“The person your mother was trying to escape from.”

Sarah swallowed.

“Who?”

The woman’s eyes filled.

“Your father.”
